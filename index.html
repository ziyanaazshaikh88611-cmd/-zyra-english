⏳ Tenses

Present Simple

Habit ya routine.

Pattern: I/you/we/they + base verb
He/she/it + verb-s/-es

I study English every day.
She studies English every day.

Past Simple

Past mein complete hua action.

Pattern: Subject + past verb

I watched a movie yesterday.
She went to school.

Future Simple

Aane wale time ki baat.

Pattern: Subject + will + base verb

I will practise tomorrow.
We will play Minecraft later.

Tip: Pehle meaning samjho, phir apni life ka sentence banao. Sirf formula ratna zaroori nahi.
